The useful bit

What do we actually help with?

Whatever is getting in the way of stability, progress or just being able to breathe a bit easier.

Making sense of the mess

Sometimes the biggest problem is not knowing where to start. We help untangle what's urgent, what's important and what can wait.

Practical next steps

Plans, forms, appointments, referrals, options and actual actions. Not just “here's a leaflet, good luck.”

Advocacy

If a system is making everything harder than it needs to be, we can help you understand it and find your way through.

Accountability

Sometimes knowing somebody will ask “did you actually do the thing?” is surprisingly useful.

Connection

Support is not always another appointment. Sometimes it's people, routine, community and getting back into life.

Finding the right support

We don't pretend VB can do everything. If someone else is better placed to help, we'll say so.

A familiar problem

The revolving door can get in the bin.

Being told to call one service, who sends you to another service, who sends you back to the first one, is not support.

If we can help, we stay involved.

If something sits outside what we do, we help you work out where it actually belongs.

Less bouncing around. More getting somewhere.

Your life. Your decisions.

We don't take over.

Support should not mean somebody else suddenly becoming the manager of your life.

We can explain options, help you think things through, stand alongside you and help you make progress.

The steering wheel stays with you.

No one-size-fits-all nonsense

We meet people where they are.

Some people arrive ready to tackle everything.

Some people arrive exhausted, suspicious, overwhelmed or fairly convinced everybody can piss off.

Both are valid starting points.

Support should fit the person in front of us, not force the person to fit the service.

One important thing

We'll back effort.

You don't need perfection

Nobody expects you to suddenly become organised, cheerful and suspiciously inspirational.

You do need participation

Talk to us. Show up where you can. Tell us when something isn't working.

Progress can be messy

Two steps forward and one step backwards is still movement.

We don't expect perfect. We do expect honest effort.

Is VB right for me?

Maybe. Let's find out.

You want practical help

You don't need another person nodding sympathetically while nothing actually changes.

You're willing to engage

Even if that starts with one message saying “I don't know where to start.”

You want support, not control

We work with people. We don't run their lives for them.

And if we're not the right fit?

We'll be honest.

We would rather help you find the right support than keep you in something that isn't useful.

Ego doesn't get a seat at the table.
