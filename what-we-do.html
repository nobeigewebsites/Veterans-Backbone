The basic idea

We help you work out what needs dealing with.

Sometimes there's one obvious problem. Sometimes there are twelve problems all fighting for first place.

We help untangle what's happening, work out what matters now, identify useful next steps and stay involved while things move forward.

One thing at a time still counts.

A particular pet hate

The revolving door can get in the bin.

Being told to ring somebody else, who tells you to contact another department, who sends you back to the first one, is not support.

We try to keep the pieces connected, stay involved where appropriate and reduce the amount of time you spend explaining the same bloody story from the beginning.

We've been there. We won't leave you spinning.

Also worth saying

We don't take over your life.

Support should increase your control, not replace it.

We can help you understand options, work through problems and stand beside you when things are difficult. The decisions about your life are still yours.

You keep the steering wheel.

And what do we expect from you?

Not perfection.

Support works best when everybody involved actually takes part. That includes us and it includes you.

Be real

Honesty

We can only work with what we know. You don't have to tell us everything immediately, but what you do tell us needs to be honest.

Take part

Participation

We'll work with you, but we can't do the whole journey on your behalf.

Keep talking

Communication

If something changes, isn't working or suddenly goes spectacularly sideways, tell us.

Try

Effort

Nobody expects perfect progress. We do expect you to engage with the things we've agreed to work on together.

Say it

Tell Us When We're Wrong

If a plan isn't useful, a suggestion makes no sense or we've misunderstood something, say so.

Human allowed

Bad Days

Bad days happen. Setbacks happen. Motivation occasionally leaves the building without notice. None of that automatically means support has failed.

This goes both ways

You get to expect things from us too.

Respect. Honesty. Clear communication. Appropriate boundaries. Safeguarding. Being treated like a human being.

We don't get to demand effort and accountability from you while conveniently forgetting our own.

Accountability is a two-way street.

Seriously

We are not looking for perfect people.

If everybody arrived organised, confident, motivated, financially stable, emotionally sorted and completely certain what to do next, there wouldn't be much point in us being here.

Progress can be slow. It can wobble. Sometimes it goes backwards before it goes forwards.

Effort matters. Perfection doesn't.

Basic ground rules

Don't be a dick.

Surprisingly effective policy.

Respect People

Staff, volunteers, other veterans, partners and anybody else involved deserve to be treated with respect.

Respect Boundaries

Support needs boundaries to be safe, useful and sustainable for everybody.

Safety Comes First

Threatening, abusive or unsafe behaviour may mean we need to change, pause or end how support is provided.

Different needs. Different routes.

Support won't look the same for everybody.

One person might need practical VB support. Another might need advocacy. Somebody else might benefit from Flamingo Rise, the RV Crew or specialist support elsewhere.

Sometimes it's a mixture.

The route follows the person.
