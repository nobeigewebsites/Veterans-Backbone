How referrals work

There isn't one
correct way in.

Refer yourself

You don't need permission from anybody else.

Another organisation

A service you're already working with can refer you.

A professional

Support workers, health professionals and others can refer where appropriate.

A mate finally loses patience

Sometimes somebody else notices you're not actually “fine”.

All routes are welcome.

Then what?

We have
a conversation.

That's genuinely it.

1

What's going on?

We start with where things are now. Not your entire autobiography.

2

What's already helping?

We don't assume everything is broken.

3

What needs to change?

We look at what you'd like to achieve and whether VB can genuinely help.

Assessment

Can I
fail it?

Nope.

This isn't school.

No pass marks. No trick questions. No gold stars.

We're assessing your situation — not your worth.

Another common question

Do I need to tell you
everything?

Absolutely not.

Trust takes time.

You don't need to hand over your entire life story on day one. Tell us enough to start safely and honestly.

And if VB isn't the right fit?

We'll
tell you.

We're not going to pretend we can do everything.

If another service is better placed to help with part of what you need, we'll be honest about it and help point you in the right direction.

Useful beats impressive.

The bottom line

We don't assess
your worth.

We assess what's happening, what support might help and what the next sensible step is.

Person first. Situation second. Paperwork somewhere after that.
