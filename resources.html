A small but important bit

External doesn't mean endorsed forever.

We link to organisations because their information or services may be useful.

Veterans Backbone doesn't control those websites, their waiting times, their eligibility rules or what they decide to change next Tuesday.

If a link is broken, outdated or sends you somewhere useless, tell us.

Still can't find it?
