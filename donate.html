Where your money goes

This is what your donation does.

No vague "making a difference" waffle. Your support helps us do actual things for actual people.

Practical support.

Helping veterans and their families navigate housing, benefits, services, referrals and the general maze of systems that somehow manage to make simple things complicated.

Advocacy.

Helping people be heard when they're exhausted, overwhelmed or getting bounced between services.

Community.

Helping create opportunities for connection, events, peer support and spaces where people don't have to explain every bloody thing from scratch.

Resources.

Developing useful information, practical tools and resources people can actually understand and use.

Keeping support accessible.

Donations help us reduce barriers so someone needing help isn't being asked to jump through another twelve hoops first.

Keeping VB running.

The boring but necessary stuff too — systems, insurance, technology, administration and everything required to keep a charity functioning properly.

Every bit counts

It doesn't have to be a huge donation.

A few quid is still a few quid we didn't have before. Lots of small donations together can fund something genuinely useful.

Small doesn't mean pointless. ♥
