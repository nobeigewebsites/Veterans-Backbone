SO WHAT ACTUALLY
IS VB?

Veterans Backbone is a veteran support initiative with a difference.

We're building a way to help veterans navigate the stuff life throws at them — support, advocacy, practical problems, rebuilding and connection.

We don't want to replace existing charities or services. Our aim is to help link the pieces together, work out what support is needed and follow things through instead of simply passing people on.

Think of the backbone we're building: support that actually joins up.

We're straight-talking, occasionally sweary and unapologetically human.

No corporate scripts. No pretending life fits neatly into boxes. We're building our approach around real people, not tick-box assumptions.

DIFFERENT BATTLEFIELDS. SHARED UNDERSTANDING.

WE SPEAK
FLUENT VETERAN.

You shouldn't have to explain every military reference, every habit, every bit of dark humour or why certain things just make sense to people who've been there.

Let's get something straight. I haven't worn the uniform. I'm not a veteran, and I would never pretend to be one. That title belongs to those who've earned it.

But I do know what it's like to fight battles nobody else can see. To deal with systems that don't join up. To have your life turned upside down and somehow be expected to figure everything out yourself.

So when I say we get it, I don't mean we've lived your military experience. I mean we understand what it's like to struggle, to rebuild and to need someone who actually gives a shit.

Veterans Backbone is being built with veterans' experiences and voices at its heart. Their military knowledge matters. Their individual stories matter. And nobody should have to explain their entire life from bloody scratch just to be heard.

Same scars.
Different battlefield.
