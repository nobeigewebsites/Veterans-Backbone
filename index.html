SO WHAT ACTUALLY
IS VB?

Veterans Backbone is a charity with a difference.

We help veterans find their way through the stuff life throws at them — support, advocacy, practical problems, rebuilding and connection.

We don't try to replace other charities or services. We help link the pieces together, work out what support is actually needed and stay alongside people while they move forward.

Think of us as the backbone holding the support together.

We're straight-talking, occasionally sweary and unapologetically human.

No corporate scripts. No pretending life fits neatly into boxes. Just real people working with the person actually standing in front of us.

NO TRANSLATION REQUIRED

WE SPEAK
FLUENT VETERAN.

You shouldn't have to explain every military reference, every habit, every bit of dark humour or why certain things just make sense to people who've been there.

Veterans Backbone isn't about pretending every veteran has the same experience.

It's about understanding enough of the language, culture and reality that you don't have to start every conversation from bloody scratch.

We get it. We've been there.
