How We Keep Our Shit Together

Privacy
& Data.

Your information matters. If we need it, there should be a proper reason.

Collect less. Explain why. Protect it properly.

Veterans Backbone is currently being established. We are not yet a registered charity, and our veteran support services have not launched.

This page explains how personal information may be handled during the project's current development stage, and how we intend to approach information governance in the future.

Who you're dealing with

First, let's be
clear about us.

Veterans Backbone is a proposed charitable organisation currently being established in England.

The project is preparing for registration as a Foundation Charitable Incorporated Organisation. That registration has not yet been completed.

During this development stage, the founder manages general project correspondence and is the contact point for questions about information received through the project.

Privacy contact: veteransbackbone@gmail.com

As the organisation is formally established, responsibility for its personal data and the identity of the relevant data controller will need to be accurately reflected in this notice.

The basic principle

We don't collect information
for the sake of it.

Information should be collected for a clear and lawful purpose.

It should be relevant, limited to what is needed, handled appropriately and not kept indefinitely just because storage is cheap.

These principles are reflected in the information governance framework being developed for Veterans Backbone.

We do not sell personal information.

Your life is not a data collection exercise.

Current website and project activity

What happens
right now?

Veterans Backbone is not providing direct veteran support or operating its proposed case management service.

We are not currently accepting support referrals, opening individual support cases or collecting donations through this website.

At this stage, personal information may be involved when someone:

  • Sends a general project enquiry by email
  • Contacts us about a proposed partnership or future involvement
  • Raises a concern about the project or its website
  • Uses an authorised member account or login service
  • Visits the website and associated technical services process information needed to deliver it

The website contact form is not currently accepting submissions.

Please do not send medical records, financial documents, identification documents, detailed safeguarding disclosures or confidential support case information through the general project email.

What information is involved?

It depends on
why you're here.

The categories of personal information involved in current project activities may include:

  • Names and email addresses provided in correspondence
  • Contact details someone chooses to share
  • Information included in general enquiries or messages
  • Relevant correspondence and responses
  • Basic information relating to proposed collaborations or project involvement
  • Account identifiers and authentication information associated with authorised logins
  • Technical information processed by website hosting and authentication services

We do not ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information as part of a general project enquiry.

If sensitive information is sent without being requested, we will need to consider how to handle it lawfully, securely and appropriately.

That may include limiting access, seeking appropriate advice or securely disposing of information that is not needed, subject to relevant legal obligations.

Why we use information

There should always
be a reason.

During the development stage, personal information may be used to:

  • Read and respond to general project enquiries
  • Discuss potential future partnerships or involvement
  • Maintain necessary correspondence records
  • Consider complaints or concerns about the project
  • Manage authorised access to account-based website features
  • Maintain website security and technical functionality
  • Meet applicable legal and data protection obligations

We are not currently using the website to assess eligibility for veteran support or to make decisions about individual support cases.

Why we're allowed to use it

Data protection law
still applies.

The UK General Data Protection Regulation and Data Protection Act 2018 govern how personal information is handled.

Personal information must have an appropriate lawful basis for each purpose for which it is used.

For current project correspondence, this may include legitimate interests in responding to enquiries and establishing the organisation, where those interests do not override individuals' rights.

Where information must be handled to comply with a legal obligation, that obligation may provide the relevant lawful basis.

Authorised account and security-related processing must also have an appropriate identified lawful basis.

Consent will only be relied upon where it is an appropriate lawful basis and the necessary requirements are met.

Health information and other special category personal data require additional legal safeguards and an applicable condition before they can be processed.

"Because we fancy keeping it" is not a lawful basis.

General correspondence

Email is useful.
But it has limits.

General project enquiries are currently directed to our project email address:

veteransbackbone@gmail.com

Messages may contain the sender's email address, name and whatever information they choose to include.

Correspondence is handled through an email service provided by Google.

Our general inbox is not a secure case management portal or an emergency response service.

Please use it for general project enquiries, not for submitting confidential case files.

Where a message raises a serious concern, it may be necessary to consider appropriate steps under applicable law and safeguarding responsibilities.

Where information may be processed

The internet doesn't stop
at the UK border.

Some technology and communication providers operate internationally.

Personal information handled through those services may involve processing or access outside the United Kingdom.

Where restricted international transfers occur, appropriate data protection requirements and applicable safeguards must be considered.

Provider arrangements, relevant destinations and transfer safeguards need to be checked and kept accurate as services change.

Sharing information

We don't chuck
your details around.

We do not sell personal information.

Personal information may be processed by providers delivering relevant website, communication or authentication services.

Information may also need to be disclosed where necessary and lawful, including to comply with a legal obligation or address a serious safety concern.

Any sharing should be necessary, proportionate and limited to information relevant to its purpose.

We do not currently operate a service coordinating individual veteran support cases between organisations.

Confidentiality has limits

Safety can
come first.

Confidentiality matters, but absolute secrecy cannot be promised in every circumstance.

Where there is a genuine serious safeguarding concern, information may sometimes need to be disclosed to an appropriate authority or professional.

Any such decision must have an appropriate lawful basis and take account of the circumstances.

The project is not currently equipped to provide individual safeguarding assessments or crisis intervention.

Safeguarding & Standards
Keeping information safe

Privacy isn't much use
if the data is left lying around.

We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse, inappropriate access and unauthorised disclosure.

The developing information governance framework includes principles of restricted access, appropriate recordkeeping, accountability and breach reporting.

Information should only be accessed by people who have a legitimate reason to handle it.

No website or communication system can guarantee absolute security.

Our information security arrangements will need ongoing review as additional systems and services become operational.

How long information is kept

Not forever just because
storage is cheap.

Personal information should not be retained longer than is necessary for its lawful purpose.

General enquiries should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage relevant follow-up or retain records required for a legitimate reason or legal obligation.

Account and technical information may be retained under the applicable arrangements of the authentication and website service providers.

Some information may need longer retention for a legal dispute, safeguarding matter or statutory obligation.

A formal retention schedule will need to define appropriate periods and review points for the organisation's different record types.

Information no longer required should be securely deleted or anonymised as appropriate.

Your data protection rights

It's your
information.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Ask whether personal information is held about you
  • Request access to and a copy of your information
  • Have inaccurate information corrected
  • Request deletion in certain circumstances
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Request data portability where that right applies
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis, without affecting earlier lawful processing

These rights are not absolute in every situation.

Requests will need to be considered under applicable data protection requirements.

Having your information doesn't mean owning your life.

Children & young people

Extra care
still matters.

Veterans Backbone is not currently delivering services to children or young people.

Where personal information relating to a child or young person is received or handled, additional care may be required under relevant data protection and safeguarding rules.

Please do not submit sensitive information about children through the general project email.

Future systems

Technology should help.
Not take over.

Veterans Backbone is developing digital systems intended to support future case management, organisational accountability and appropriate recordkeeping.

These systems are not currently being used to accept or manage individual veteran support cases.

Any future operational case management system will need appropriate access controls, data protection assessments, record retention, privacy information and safeguards.

Artificial intelligence may be considered for appropriate future workflows.

It must not bypass permissions, confidentiality, safeguarding decisions or accountable human judgement.

Decisions affecting people's support and safety must remain appropriately accountable to human decision-makers.

Humans first. Clever software second.

If something goes wrong

Problems don't improve
by hiding them.

An actual or suspected personal data breach should be assessed, contained and appropriately recorded.

Where the law requires notification to the Information Commissioner's Office or affected individuals, the relevant reporting requirements and timescales must be followed.

Breach handling and escalation arrangements form part of the information governance framework being developed.

Concerns about how the project has handled personal information can be raised through our project email.

Questions about your data?

Ask us.

If you want to ask about personal information held in connection with Veterans Backbone, request access or correction, or raise a data protection concern, please contact:

veteransbackbone@gmail.com

For ordinary personal data requests, the normal legal response period is one month, subject to the applicable rules and permitted extensions.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator.
