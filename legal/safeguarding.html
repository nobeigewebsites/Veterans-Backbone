How We Keep Our Shit Together

Safeguarding
& Standards.

Looking after people properly matters more than looking impressive.

Safe people. Clear boundaries. No hiding problems.

Veterans Backbone is currently being established. We are not yet a registered charity or an operational veteran support service.

We have developed draft safeguarding and governance frameworks. The arrangements required for delivering support will be reviewed, formally approved and put into practice before services begin.

What safeguarding means to us

People come first.

Veterans Backbone is being established to support former members of the UK Armed Forces who may be experiencing hardship, disadvantage or difficult circumstances.

Some people may be dealing with trauma, isolation, illness, disability, financial difficulties or several problems at once.

None of those circumstances should mean somebody loses their dignity, their choices or their right to be safe.

Safeguarding is about recognising and responding appropriately to abuse, neglect, exploitation, harassment, unsafe behaviour and other risks of harm.

It also means having the appropriate people, training, procedures and accountability in place to act when something goes wrong.

If something feels wrong, it deserves to be taken seriously.

Where things currently stand

Foundations first.
Services second.

Veterans Backbone is not currently accepting veteran support cases, referrals or requests for safeguarding assessments.

Our safeguarding policies and operational arrangements are being prepared for review and approval by the intended trustees.

Before direct support begins, the organisation intends to establish clear safeguarding responsibilities, suitable training, reporting procedures, escalation arrangements and appropriate recordkeeping.

A responsible safeguarding lead or equivalent arrangement will need to be identified before the service becomes operational.

We won't pretend those arrangements are operational before they actually are.

Do it properly. Not just on paper.

Who this will apply to

Everyone means
everyone.

Once the relevant services and activities are established, safeguarding responsibilities will extend to everyone working within their scope.

  • Veterans receiving support
  • Staff and support workers
  • Volunteers
  • Charity trustees
  • Contractors and advisers
  • Community and RV Crew participants
  • People attending future activities or events
  • Partners acting within agreed arrangements
  • Any future approved affiliated sites

This list describes the intended scope of the framework. It does not mean those roles, programmes or sites already exist.

Nobody should get a free pass because of their title, friendship, reputation or position within an organisation.

Our core principles

The rules aren't
complicated.

These are the principles underpinning the safeguarding framework we're developing.

Respect People

Treat people with dignity. Listen to them and take their concerns seriously.

Respect Boundaries

Personal and professional boundaries help keep relationships safe.

Don't Exploit People

Vulnerability, dependence, personal information or positions of trust must never be misused.

Speak Up

Concerns should be raised, recorded appropriately and not quietly buried.

Protect Information

Personal information must be handled lawfully and shared only where there is an appropriate reason.

Act When Something Is Unsafe

Safeguarding concerns must be taken seriously. Protecting people matters more than avoiding embarrassment.

Boundaries

Being human doesn't mean
having no boundaries.

Veterans Backbone is deliberately being developed with a human, straight-talking approach.

That doesn't mean anything goes.

Future staff, volunteers and anyone representing the organisation will be expected to maintain appropriate boundaries.

The framework is intended to address exploitation, inappropriate dependency, favouritism, conflicts of interest, confidentiality and conduct.

Safeguarding doesn't stop being important because a conversation feels informal or friendly.

Human doesn't mean careless.

Recognising concerns

We don't want problems
swept under the rug.

The future safeguarding framework is designed to recognise concerns arising from situations such as:

  • Abuse, neglect or exploitation
  • Threats, harassment or intimidation
  • Inappropriate relationships or boundary violations
  • Unsafe environments or practices
  • Concerns about a person's immediate safety
  • Misuse of personal or confidential information
  • Failures to report or respond to serious concerns

Where the service becomes operational, concerns will need to be considered by appropriately responsible people, with proportionate recording, escalation and referral.

No staff member or volunteer should be expected to manage serious safeguarding decisions alone.

Real accountability

People make decisions.
Not just systems.

Veterans Backbone is developing digital systems intended to support its future operations.

Technology may help organise information, prompt appropriate checks or record actions.

It must not replace accountable human judgement on safeguarding, determine someone's safety independently or bypass required permissions and oversight.

Any future use of automation or artificial intelligence must respect confidentiality, privacy, safeguarding requirements and human accountability.

A computer doesn't get the final say on somebody's safety.

Important information

Worried about somebody
right now?

Veterans Backbone is not an emergency response service and is not currently providing individual safeguarding assessments or veteran support.

If somebody is in immediate danger or there is an emergency, call 999.

For non-emergency adult safeguarding concerns, contact the relevant local council adult safeguarding service or an appropriate established organisation.

Please don't wait for Veterans Backbone to launch before seeking help.

Our general project email is not an emergency contact point and is not monitored as a crisis service.

Concerned about Veterans Backbone?

You are allowed to
challenge us too.

Safeguarding and accountability aren't just things we expect from other organisations.

Even while Veterans Backbone is being established, concerns about the project's conduct, communications, privacy or the behaviour of someone representing it should be taken seriously.

If you need to raise a concern about Veterans Backbone itself, you can contact us through the general project email.

Please describe the general nature of your concern initially. Don't send medical records, confidential case files or other sensitive documents through ordinary email.

If sensitive information is necessary to examine a concern, an appropriate way of handling that information will need to be considered.

Raising a genuine concern in good faith should not result in retaliation.

Future affiliated sites

Local doesn't mean
unaccountable.

Veterans Backbone has longer-term ambitions to develop a model that could allow appropriate local organisations to operate within agreed shared standards.

There are currently no licensed Veterans Backbone sites, and no licensing scheme is operational.

If such arrangements are introduced in the future, safeguarding responsibilities, escalation, monitoring and intervention powers will need to be clearly documented and agreed.

The aim would be local independence with appropriate accountability, not a free-for-all or unnecessary interference.

Guardianship. Not domination.

Our Future Sites Model
Standards & accountability

Being different doesn't mean
making it up as we go along.

Veterans Backbone is preparing for registration as a Foundation Charitable Incorporated Organisation.

Under the proposed structure, charity trustees will hold ultimate responsibility for the organisation's governance, finances, risks and safeguarding.

Appropriate responsibilities may be delegated to staff or specialist leads once appointed, but trustee accountability cannot simply be delegated away.

Safeguarding concerns, governance decisions and serious risks must be dealt with under the appropriate arrangements rather than ignored or hidden.

Formal responsibilities will take effect in accordance with the organisation's legal establishment and approved governance arrangements.

Accountability is not optional.

Governance documents

The grown-up
paperwork.

Veterans Backbone has been developing its governing constitution, safeguarding policies, financial controls and operational frameworks.

These documents form part of the preparation for registration and future service delivery.

Draft documents should not be mistaken for formally adopted policies or evidence that services are already operating.

In preparation

Financial Governance

In preparation

Safeguarding Procedures

Service-level procedures, training and named responsibilities must be ready before support launches.

Public copies of appropriate governing documents and policies may be made available once their status and publication versions are confirmed.
