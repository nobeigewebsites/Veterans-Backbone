How We Keep Our Shit Together

Cookies.

The website kind. Not the chocolate-chip kind.

Small files. Boring name. Occasionally useful.

We believe people should know when a website uses cookies or similar technologies, what they're for and what choices are available.

Veterans Backbone is currently being established, and our website is still under construction.

First things first

What the bloody hell
are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website or online service can store on your device through your browser.

They can help a website remember certain information, maintain a login session or support security features.

Some cookies are temporary and disappear when a browsing session ends.

Others can remain on your device for longer.

Websites may also use similar technologies, including browser storage and other methods of storing or accessing information on a device.

Those technologies can be subject to similar privacy requirements.

Technology should have a purpose. Not just exist.

The website right now

What are we
actually using?

Veterans Backbone currently publishes its public website using GitHub Pages.

Most public pages are informational and do not require visitors to log in.

The website also has member login functionality using Clerk authentication technology.

This means cookies or similar browser storage may be used when someone accesses the login service or an account-based area.

We are not currently operating website donation payments, an online support referral system or an active general enquiry form.

Those future features should not be confused with services that are already available.

Say what exists. Not what looks impressive.

Essential functionality

Some things simply
need to work.

Some cookies or similar technologies may be necessary to provide a feature that someone has specifically requested.

For example, authentication technologies may be needed to establish or maintain a secure login session.

Security-related technologies may also be needed to protect accounts or prevent misuse.

Where a technology meets the legal requirements for an applicable exception, prior cookie consent may not be required.

That does not remove the responsibility to explain its use appropriately.

Something doesn't become essential just because a website owner finds it convenient.

Member Login

Logging in needs
a bit of technology.

The Veterans Backbone member login currently uses Clerk, a third-party authentication provider.

When someone accesses the login or account features, Clerk may use cookies or other browser storage to support authentication, sessions and security.

Depending on the configuration, this may involve information being exchanged with Clerk's services.

Member login is intended for authorised accounts. Public membership applications are not yet open.

Login functionality does not mean Veterans Backbone's veteran support service is operational.

A login is not a licence to snoop.

Third-party technology

Sometimes somebody else
does the technical bit.

Parts of the website rely on external services.

GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages is used to publish and deliver the public website.

Hosting infrastructure may process technical information related to website access and service delivery.

This does not mean that every visitor is automatically given a tracking cookie.

Clerk

Clerk provides the authentication technology used on the member login pages.

It may use browser storage and cookies to support sessions, authentication and security.

The exact technologies depend on the active configuration.

Future Services

We may introduce additional website features in the future.

These could include forms, approved fundraising features, events or other account services.

They are not currently part of an operational veteran support service.

External services may have their own privacy information and technical arrangements.

We will need to review any additional technologies before introducing them.

Optional technologies

Optional should
actually mean optional.

Some websites use cookies or similar technologies for advertising, profiling, marketing or tracking visitors across websites.

If Veterans Backbone introduces technologies for purposes that require consent, visitors must be given clear information and an appropriate choice before those technologies are used.

Consent must involve a genuine, informed choice where it is legally required.

Continuing to browse a website does not, by itself, establish valid consent.

Some narrowly defined statistical or appearance-related uses may qualify for specific legal exceptions.

Those exceptions have their own conditions, including appropriate information and a simple way to object.

Any exception must genuinely apply to the technology and its actual purpose.

No sneaky yes-by-default nonsense.

Your choices

Your browser has
controls too.

Most modern browsers let you view, block or delete cookies and other stored website information.

These settings are normally available through your browser's privacy or site information controls.

Blocking or deleting essential authentication information may cause login sessions to end or prevent account features from working properly.

Browser controls are useful, but they do not replace a website's responsibility to provide consent or objection mechanisms where the law requires them.

If non-exempt optional tracking technologies are introduced, an appropriate consent mechanism must be provided before they are activated.

Still building

Keep it simple
until there's a reason not to.

The Veterans Backbone website is currently under development.

We aim to avoid unnecessary tracking and additional browser storage where it serves no useful purpose.

As the website changes, we will need to review any technologies used by new features.

That includes authentication, forms, embedded content, optional analytics and future payment or fundraising services.

Where new technologies introduce different privacy requirements, this information and any necessary website controls will need to be updated.

More features shouldn't mean less privacy.

Cookies & privacy

The two
overlap.

Some cookies or similar technologies may involve personal information.

Where that happens, applicable data protection requirements also apply.

That includes having appropriate reasons for processing personal information and explaining what happens to it.

Our Privacy & Data page covers the wider handling of personal information connected with the project.
