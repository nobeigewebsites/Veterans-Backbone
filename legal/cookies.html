How We Keep Our Shit Together

Cookies

The website kind. Not the chocolate-chip kind.

Small files. Boring name. Occasionally useful.

What are cookies?

Tiny bits of information stored by your browser.

Cookies are small text files that websites can place on your device.

They can help websites remember things, keep services working properly or understand how a website is being used.

Some cookies disappear when you close your browser. Others may remain for longer.

How Veterans Backbone may use them

We only need them where they serve a purpose.

The Veterans Backbone website may use cookies or similar technologies for things such as:

  • Keeping parts of the website working properly
  • Remembering basic preferences
  • Supporting login or authentication features
  • Helping forms or other services function
  • Protecting the website from misuse or security threats
  • Understanding how visitors use the website

We do not want the website stuffed with unnecessary tracking.

Essential cookies

Some things simply need to work.

Certain cookies may be necessary for website features to operate properly.

These can include things connected with security, forms, account sessions or authentication.

Where a cookie is genuinely essential, the website may need to use it without asking for optional consent first.

Optional cookies

Optional means optional.

If Veterans Backbone introduces analytics, advertising, marketing or other non-essential tracking, those technologies should be treated differently from essential cookies.

Where consent is legally required, visitors should be given a meaningful choice before those cookies are used.

No sneaky yes-by-default nonsense.

Third-party services

Sometimes another service is doing the technical bit.

Parts of the Veterans Backbone website may rely on third-party platforms or services.

These might include services used for:

  • Website hosting
  • Forms
  • Member login or authentication
  • Donations
  • Embedded content
  • Analytics, if introduced

Those services may set their own cookies or process technical information when you use the relevant feature.

Member features

Logging in may require cookies.

If Veterans Backbone uses a member login system, cookies or similar storage may be needed to keep somebody signed in and maintain a secure session.

These are normally connected with the functioning and security of the member area rather than advertising or marketing.

Your choices

Your browser has controls too.

Most browsers allow you to view, remove or block cookies through their settings.

Blocking all cookies may affect parts of some websites, particularly features that rely on sessions, authentication or saved preferences.

If Veterans Backbone introduces a cookie consent tool, visitors will also be able to use that tool to manage relevant non-essential choices.

Our current approach

Keep it simple until there is a reason not to.

Veterans Backbone aims to minimise unnecessary cookies and tracking.

As the website develops, this page should be updated whenever new services are added that introduce additional cookies or tracking technologies.

That includes reviewing any services used for member accounts, forms, donations, embedded media or analytics.

Cookies & privacy

The two overlap.

Where cookie information can identify somebody, or is connected with other personal information, data protection rules may also apply.
