Get Involved

You don't have to wear a cape.

Veterans Backbone needs different people, different skills and different levels of involvement.

Some people have hours to give every week. Some can help once in a while. Some bring lived experience. Some bring professional skills. Some have a car and don't mind driving.

All useful.

Fancy helping?

There isn't just one way in.

Volunteering shouldn't mean squeezing yourself into a role that doesn't suit your life.

So we'll have different ways to get involved, from regular roles to occasional help.

Volunteering

Pick the kind of useful that suits you.

Exact roles will change as Veterans Backbone grows, but these are the kinds of people we'll need.

Lived Experience

Veterans & ex-service volunteers

People who've lived some of this stuff themselves and understand that support doesn't always need a clipboard and a scripted conversation.

Peer support, community activities, mentoring and helping people feel less alone may all become part of these roles.

Community

General volunteers

You don't have to be a veteran to be useful here.

Community volunteers may help with events, practical tasks, fundraising, activities, organising and all the other things that keep something like VB moving.

On The Road

Volunteer drivers

Transport can be the difference between somebody being able to access something and simply not going.

As services develop, we'll need people willing to help with transport where appropriate.

Events

Event volunteers

Helping set things up, welcome people, keep activities running and clear up afterwards when everyone else has mysteriously vanished.

Behind The Scenes

Admin & practical support

Emails, organisation, resources, event planning and the hundred tiny jobs nobody sees but absolutely notice when they haven't been done.

Bring Your Skills

Skilled volunteers

People with useful professional or practical skills may be able to support specific projects.

That could eventually include everything from trades and technology to fundraising, communications, training and specialist knowledge.

When You Can

Flexible & occasional volunteers

Can't commit every week? Fine.

Some roles may only need help at particular events, busy periods or one-off projects.

Regular Help

Part-time volunteering

For people who want something a little more regular, we'll eventually have roles with agreed hours or recurring responsibilities.

Raise Some Chaos

Fundraising volunteers

Events, challenges, community fundraising and creative ideas that help bring money in without making everybody want to hide.

Worried you're not qualified?

Useful beats impressive.

Some roles will need specific experience, training or checks.

Plenty won't.

Reliability, kindness, common sense, willingness to learn and actually turning up count for quite a lot around here.

The sensible bit

Some roles need checks.

Depending on the role, volunteering with Veterans Backbone may involve references, safeguarding requirements, appropriate training or background checks.

That's not us being dramatic. It's us taking people's safety seriously.

Safeguarding
Careers

Paid roles will live here too.

As Veterans Backbone grows, there may be paid roles available alongside volunteering.

No vacancies right now.

When paid roles become available, we'll publish them here with proper job descriptions, hours, salary and application information.

No mysterious "competitive salary" bullshit if we can help it.

Something for later

Colleges, students & placements.

This isn't running yet, but we'd eventually like to explore working with colleges, universities and training providers.

That could mean appropriate placements, volunteering opportunities, project work or giving students useful real-world experience alongside their studies.

We'll build this properly when Veterans Backbone is ready to support it properly.

Good idea. Just not half-arsed. ♥

Organisations

Want to work with us instead?

Businesses, community groups, charities, training providers and other organisations may be able to support Veterans Backbone in different ways too.

Partnerships

Work together on something useful without forming a committee about the committee first.

Skills & Services

Offer expertise, equipment, professional support or practical help to a particular project.

Fundraising & Sponsorship

Support an event, campaign, project or something else that actually needs funding.

Interested?

Tell us what you could bring.

You don't need to choose the perfect role before getting in touch.

Tell us a bit about yourself, what you're interested in and roughly how much time you'd like to give.
