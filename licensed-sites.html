Licensed Veterans Backbone Sites

Same backbone. Local people. Local decisions.

Veterans Backbone is designed to grow without turning every site into a clone run from somewhere else.

Local sites keep their own leadership, staff, services and day-to-day decisions.

Veterans Backbone protects the standards, safeguarding and integrity behind the name.

Build it properly first. Grow it second.

So what is one?

A local organisation under the VB umbrella.

A licensed Veterans Backbone site is locally run but operates within the shared standards, safeguarding expectations and values attached to the Veterans Backbone name.

The local team runs the site.

Veterans Backbone protects the framework around it.

Rules of the road. Not hands on the steering wheel.

Local autonomy

The site runs the site.

Licensed sites are not expected to phone headquarters every time somebody needs a new box of teabags.

Local Leadership

Each site has its own local leadership and management structure.

Local Staff

Sites choose and manage their own staff within agreed standards.

Local Services

Sites decide how services are delivered and what works best for their local community.

Local Budgets

Sites manage their own budgets, suppliers and routine spending.

Local Partnerships

Sites build their own relationships with organisations in their area.

Local Culture

Every site can have its own personality, provided it stays inside the shared VB standards.

What Veterans Backbone protects

Freedom doesn't mean free-for-all.

Using the Veterans Backbone name means agreeing to protect the things the name is supposed to stand for.

Safeguarding

Safe practice isn't optional.

Ethical Standards

People come before ego, reputation theatre or convenience.

Framework Standards

Sites have room to work differently without drifting away from what VB is.

Integrity

The name means something. We intend to keep it that way.

Continuity

If something threatens safe or stable delivery, it needs dealing with early.

Accountability

Problems don't disappear because everybody would rather not talk about them.

Also important

We don't run your site for you.

Veterans Backbone does not exist to micromanage local teams.

We don't approve your everyday spending.

We don't choose your suppliers.

We don't manage your staff.

We don't design every local programme.

We don't expect every site to look identical.

Guardianship. Not domination.

When does the umbrella step in?

When something actually matters.

Oversight is supportive by default.

Formal intervention only happens when agreed thresholds are crossed.

Safeguarding risks aren't being managed.

Serious ethical concerns appear.

The site repeatedly drifts from shared standards.

People feel unable to raise concerns safely.

There is a serious continuity or insolvency risk.

Legal or regulatory problems emerge.

Data is being handled unsafely.

The aim is always to correct the problem, protect people and restore safe local independence.

How affiliation works

Clear expectations. Both ways.

A licensed site relationship is built around a formal guardianship affiliation agreement.

01

Talk

We start with a proper conversation about what you're trying to build and why.

02

Check Alignment

We look at safeguarding, governance, culture and whether the model actually fits.

03

Agree The Framework

Both sides agree responsibilities, standards, reporting and escalation routes.

04

Operate Locally

The local team runs the site within the shared framework.

Money & independence

Your money stays your responsibility.

Licensed sites manage their own budgets and bank accounts.

Veterans Backbone sets minimum financial standards and may ask for summary-level information where assurance is needed.

The umbrella doesn't hold local site bank accounts and doesn't control routine spending.

Interested in becoming a VB site?

Want to use the Veterans Backbone name?

We're open to conversations with people and organisations who believe in the same basic idea:

local support, human delivery, strong safeguarding, honest governance and no unnecessary bullshit.

Becoming a licensed site won't mean buying a logo and carrying on however you like.

It also won't mean handing over control of your organisation.

The right fit sits somewhere sensible in the middle.

Could this be you?

We care more about fit than labels.

Minimum expectations

The name comes with responsibilities.

Safeguarding First

Proper safeguarding structures, reporting and action.

Transparent Governance

Decisions can be explained, challenged and traced.

Financial Controls

Clear, responsible and proportionate financial management.

Respectful Culture

People must be able to speak up without fear or retaliation.

Honest Communication

Risks and problems get raised early, not hidden until they explode.

Shared Purpose

The work must genuinely support veterans and their families.

And if it stops working?

Nobody is chained to the furniture.

Affiliation isn't supposed to trap either side.

If alignment breaks down and can't be repaired, the relationship can end through an agreed exit process, while making sure safeguarding and continuity are dealt with properly.

Fair in. Fair out.
