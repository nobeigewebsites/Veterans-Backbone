How it works

No obstacle course required.

The process is deliberately simple.

1

Talk

Tell us what's going on. One sentence is enough to start.

2

Work it out

We look at what's happening, what already helps and what might need tackling.

3

Make a plan

A useful one. Not a twenty-page masterpiece nobody reads.

4

Keep going

Things change. Support changes with them. We don't vanish after the paperwork.

Important bit

You can't fail at needing help.

This isn't school. There are no grades, pass marks or gold stars.

We're trying to understand your situation — not decide whether you're worthy of support.

Person first. Problem second.

The whole point

No revolving doors.

No bouncing you between people until you give up.

No pretending a phone number and “good luck” counts as support.

If we can help, we'll work alongside you. If somebody else is better placed to help with part of it, we'll tell you and help you find them.

Support that stays put.
