How it works

No obstacle course
required.

The process is deliberately simple.

1

Talk

Tell us what's going on. One sentence is enough to start.

2

Work it out

We look at what's happening, what already helps and what might need tackling.

3

Make a plan

A useful one. Not a twenty-page masterpiece nobody reads.

4

Keep going

Things change. Support changes with them. We don't vanish after the paperwork.

Important bit

You can't fail at
needing help.

This isn't school. There are no grades, pass marks or gold stars.

We're trying to understand your situation — not decide whether you're worthy of support.

Person first. Problem second.

The whole point

No revolving
doors.

No bouncing you between people until you give up.

No pretending a phone number and “good luck” counts as support.

If we can help, we'll work alongside you.

If somebody else is better placed to deal with part of it, we'll be honest about that and help work out the right route.

Support that stays put.

Also worth knowing

We can't promise to
fix everything.

Some things take time. Some systems are slow. Some decisions won't go the way anybody wanted. And sometimes life changes the plan before we've finished the first one.

What we can do is be honest, work with what's actually happening and change the approach when something clearly isn't working.

No miracle cure. No bullshit.

Still not sure?

That's fine.
Start anyway.

You don't need perfect words, a diagnosis, a crisis or your entire life story prepared.

“I don't know where to start” is a perfectly acceptable place to start.
