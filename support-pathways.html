Our Planned Support Approach

Support
pathways.

Support doesn't have to mean one service, one appointment or one very depressing waiting room.

Different people. Different routes.

The pathways below describe our proposed model. Veterans Backbone is not yet operational, and these are not services currently available through us.

First things first

There isn't one
fixed pathway.

What works for one person may be completely useless for another.

We want our future service to look at what's happening, what support already exists, what may be missing and which route makes sense for that individual.

That would involve appropriate assessment, consent, safeguarding and realistic service boundaries.

Person first. Pathway second.

Specialist support

Other Services

VB isn't going to pretend one organisation can do absolutely everything.

Our aim is eventually to help veterans identify appropriate existing services, seek specialist advice where needed and avoid getting lost between different organisations.

Very normal, actually

A Bit Of Everything

Real life rarely fits neatly into one box.

A person's circumstances may involve practical support, advocacy, community activities and external specialist help at different points.

The proposed model is designed to recognise those connections without promising that every service can be supplied by VB.

Support shouldn't stop the second somebody is technically “not in crisis anymore.”

There can be a massive gap between surviving something and actually building a life you want afterwards.

Flamingo Rise is being developed separately around rebuilding confidence, identity, goals and personal direction.

Support without calling it support

The
RV Crew.

Humans are annoyingly social creatures.

Isolation can make everything harder, and sometimes joining people for something ordinary can be valuable too.

We're developing the idea of the RV Crew around connection, community, experiences, laughter and remembering there's more to life than whatever brought someone to VB.

This is a future initiative, not a group currently offering membership or activities.

Serious purpose. Not serious every second.

Also important

You shouldn't have to pick
your own pathway.

People shouldn't need to arrive already knowing whether they require advocacy, practical help, community support or something else.

Once operational, our intention is to assess appropriate needs together and identify realistic routes within the support available.

But VB isn't currently accepting enquiries for individual assessment or support.

“Everything's a mess” is an acceptable starting point.

Nothing is carved in stone

Your pathway
can change.

What somebody needs at the beginning may not be what they need six months later.

A person might initially require practical assistance, later need advocacy, and eventually focus more on community or specialist services.

Life can also throw another problem into the mix and support needs can increase again.

Our planned approach allows for review rather than treating an initial plan as permanent.

Support follows reality.

And this can grow

More pathways can
be added.

Veterans Backbone is being designed to connect useful support, not pretend one organisation should own all of it.

As appropriate programmes, partnerships and specialist arrangements are established, new pathways could become part of the model.

Our initial community-based service remains the priority before any wider expansion.

Build what is useful. Bin what isn't.
