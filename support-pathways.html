First things first

fixed pathway.

A pathway beyond crisis

Flamingo Rise

There can be a massive gap between surviving something and actually building a life you want afterwards.

Flamingo Rise gives us a route for that next stage: confidence, identity, goals, rebuilding and working out what comes next.

Support without calling it support

The
RV Crew.

Humans are annoyingly social creatures.

Isolation can make everything harder, and sometimes joining people for something completely ordinary does more good than another hour talking about what's wrong.

The RV Crew is about connection, community, experiences, laughter and remembering there is more to life than whatever brought you to VB.

Serious purpose. Not serious every second.

Also important

You do not have to pick
your own pathway.

You don't need to arrive knowing whether you need advocacy, coaching, community support or something else entirely.

Start with the problem. We'll work out the route with you.

“Everything's a mess” is an acceptable starting point.

Nothing is carved in stone

Your pathway
can change.

What you need at the beginning may not be what you need six months later.

Somebody may begin with intensive practical support, move into advocacy, later use coaching and eventually mainly stay connected through community.

Or life might throw another problem into the mix and support needs increase again.

Support follows reality.

And this can grow

More pathways can
be added.

Veterans Backbone is being built to connect useful support, not pretend one organisation should own all of it.

As new programmes, partners and specialist routes are properly established, they can be added here.

Build what is useful. Bin what isn't.
