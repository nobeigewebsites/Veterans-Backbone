CHANGE CONTROL

When policy changes

1
Review source

Confirm the approved policy wording and version.

2
Review requirements

Identify requirements that have changed, appeared or been removed.

3
Assess implementation

Determine whether workflows, rules, permissions, assurance or human processes need to change.

4
Approve & implement

Record authorised decisions and implementation actions.

5
Verify

Confirm that the intended policy requirement is actually reflected in practice.

🧠
Policy Brain connects the dots. It does not invent policy.

Approved source documents remain authoritative. The system can structure requirements, expose implementation gaps and connect requirements to controls, but changes to policy and significant interpretation remain governed human decisions.
