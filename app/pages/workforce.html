Administrator does not mean unrestricted human access.

Technical privileges and operational need are different things. Access to sensitive records should remain purpose-limited, attributable and controlled even where a user performs system administration.

🦄
Clear responsibility without creating a surveillance culture.

Workforce oversight should help people understand their responsibilities, keep access appropriate and identify work requiring attention. It should not become a league table of staff or an automated judgement of performance.
