RESPONSIBLE REPORTING

What we deliberately do not do

×
No veteran scoring

People are not ranked by engagement, complexity or perceived success.

×
No staff league tables

Activity data should not become an automated judgement of staff performance.

×
No outcome theatre

Reporting should not cherry-pick numbers simply because they look impressive.

×
No hidden automated decisions

Reports can inform people. They do not quietly become decision-making engines.
