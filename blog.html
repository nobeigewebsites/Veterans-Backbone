One small promise

We're not writing
for algorithms.

We're writing for humans.

That means useful information, proper stories and actual opinions rather than stuffing every sentence with keywords until nobody can stand reading it.

We also believe that people's experiences deserve to be treated with respect.

We won't publish somebody's personal story without their informed permission or treat difficult experiences as entertainment.

Humans first. Always. ♥
