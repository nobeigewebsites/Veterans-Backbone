Need help right now?

Veterans Backbone is not yet providing support services and is not an emergency or crisis response service.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999.

If you need urgent medical help but it is not an immediate emergency, contact NHS 111 in England.

If you need someone to talk to, Samaritans can be contacted free on 116 123.

Please contact an established support service for help with individual needs while Veterans Backbone is being developed.
