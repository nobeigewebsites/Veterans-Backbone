Here's the honest bit

Getting support doesn't magically
fix your life.

Some things can be sorted quickly. Some take months. Some take years. And some things may never look exactly how you wanted them to.

There will probably be setbacks. Systems will still be frustrating. Other people will still make their own choices. And occasionally life will throw another pile of shit on top while you're still dealing with the first one.

We can't promise easy. We can promise honest.

Roughly speaking

It might look something
like this.

Not necessarily in this order. Because apparently life doesn't read flowcharts.

Start somewhere

Talk

Tell us what's happening. You don't need the whole story neatly organised first.

Work it out

Make A Plan

Decide what actually matters, what can wait and what needs dealing with now.

Do the stuff

Take Action

Calls, forms, appointments, conversations, changes or whatever the next useful step happens to be.

Inevitable

Hit A Wobble

Something doesn't work. Circumstances change. Motivation disappears. Welcome to being human.

Not failure

Adjust

Work out what changed, what isn't helping and whether the plan needs changing too.

Keep building

Move Forward

However slowly. However imperfectly. Forward is still forward.

Nobody likes this bit

Sometimes you will
go backwards.

You can be doing brilliantly and then housing changes, money goes wrong, health takes a hit, a relationship falls apart or something completely unexpected lands in your lap.

Needing more support again doesn't erase the progress you already made.

It just means circumstances changed.

Wobbling isn't starting again.

Another honest bit

We can't do it
for you.

We can support you. We can advocate. We can help untangle things. We can stand beside you, help find options and call bullshit when bullshit needs calling.

But we can't make every decision go your way, control other people or make changes on your behalf that only you can make.

This has to be something we work on together.

Beside you. Not instead of you.

Progress isn't just the big stuff

Sometimes progress looks
surprisingly boring.

Answering The Phone

When last month you couldn't face it.

Turning Up

Even when every part of you wanted to cancel.

Saying “I Need Help”

Before everything completely falls apart.

Saying No

When something isn't right for you.

Sorting One Thing

Even though another eleven are still waiting.

Having A Better Week

Without demanding that it immediately becomes a better life.

Where are we trying to get to?

Hopefully, somewhere
you need us less.

The goal isn't to make you dependent on Veterans Backbone forever.

It's more stability, more confidence, more control, better connections and a life that isn't permanently organised around surviving the latest crisis.

You might still stay involved. You might join the RV Crew, come to events, help somebody else or simply disappear off and get on with your life.

Support is the bridge. Not the destination.

Bottom line

We're not promising
to fix you.

Firstly, you're a person, not a broken toaster.

Secondly, life is complicated and anybody promising that one service can solve everything is talking bollocks.

What we can do is start where you actually are, work with what's in front of us and keep adjusting when reality changes.

No miracle cure. No bullshit. Just the next useful step.
