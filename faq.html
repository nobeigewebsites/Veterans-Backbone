Veterans Backbone is being established to help veterans navigate life when it's gone a bit sideways.

Our first goal is a community-based support service that can offer practical guidance, help people navigate existing services, connect the dots and follow things through.

We're not looking to replace organisations already doing good work. We want to help make those services easier to navigate.

In short: we're building something to help join the dots when life has scattered them all over the bloody floor.

But we're not operational yet, so we aren't currently accepting support cases.

We're developing the governance, safeguarding arrangements, operational procedures and other foundations needed to do things properly.

Until registration is confirmed, we won't describe ourselves as a registered charity.

Although, registered or not, we still have absolutely no intention of becoming beige.
